Rooms

Every room on the robot's map. Untick a room to remove its fan from Apple Home, and choose how many passes each room gets. "Default" follows the Passes setting below.

"Create a fan for every room" is turned off below, so no room fans are created.

    Routines

    The routines you made in the Roborock app. Tick a routine to add a switch for it to Apple Home, untick it to remove the switch. Turning the switch on starts the routine; turning it off sends the robot back to the dock. A routine started from the Roborock app shows on its switch too.

      Room combinations

      Each combination is one more fan in Apple Home that cleans the selected rooms together.

        New combination

        A different name makes a new fan in Apple Home (its room and automations start over). To only rename it, rename the fan in the Home app.
